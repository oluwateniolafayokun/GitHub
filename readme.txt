User Story Map Lab

General Thought Process 
Before developing my story map, I started by thinking about the average experience of a person using a grocery delivery app like Instacart or Uber Eats. I tried to focus on what the person or customer would need to get done from the beginning of the process through the end of the experience. 
The map was organized around what I thought to be the three main stages of the customer's process. The three main stages were Browse Groceries, Place Order, and Receive Order. These were the three main epics in the map. I then broke each epic into smaller steps that represented major activities that the customer would partake in.

Decisions made when creating the stories
Decision 1: Breaking the larger activities into smaller steps and stories.

Why I made this decision: 
I decided to break larger activities into smaller steps and individual stories because I believed larger requirements would be difficult to understand or organize if they were described by only one card. Breaking them down allowed each card to represent a more specific action that the customer would need to perform.

How the result is captured?
The map contains 9 steps, with 3 steps under each of the 3 epics. Each step is then divided into 3 individuals tasks 

Decision 2: Focusing the stories on customer goals and actions.

Why I made this decision?
I decided to describe the stories in actions that a person might have when using the application. 

How the result is captured?
The individual stories/ tasks describe the person or customer's actions as Search for groceries, Filter products by category, select product quantity.
